Common Questions

Everything you need to know about our SMS service.

How long does it take to get an SMS?
Most SMS codes arrive within 10-60 seconds. If you don't receive a code within 5 minutes, you can cancel the number and your funds will be automatically returned to your wallet.
Are these numbers recycled?
We provide fresh, private sessions for each user. Once you use a number for a specific service, that exact number is generally not sold to another person for the same service for a set period to ensure your account security.
My payment hasn't reflected?
Payments via Flutterwave are usually instant. If your wallet hasn't been credited within 10 minutes, please send your transaction reference or receipt to our WhatsApp support for manual verification.
Which services do you support?
We support WhatsApp, Telegram, Google, Facebook, Instagram, ChatGPT, Twitter, and over 500 other international platforms.
