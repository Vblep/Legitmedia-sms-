Privacy Policy

Security & Data Protection

Data Collection

We collect minimal information required to provide SMS services: your email address for account security and transaction records for wallet management. We do not store your physical location or private browsing data.

SMS & Privacy

The SMS content received on our virtual numbers is displayed only on your private dashboard. While we monitor system logs for security, we do not share the content of your verification codes with any third-party marketing agencies.

Payment Security

All financial transactions are processed through encrypted gateways (Flutterwave). Legit Media does not store your credit card details or bank login credentials on our servers.

Cookies & Analytics

We use cookies to keep you logged in and to analyze site performance. You can disable these in your browser settings, though some dashboard features may stop functioning.
